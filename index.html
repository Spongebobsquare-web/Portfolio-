Hi, my name is Sophia Egoavil, and I am a Finance major. I am taking this course because I want to get a better understanding of Artificial Intelligence and how it is becoming a bigger part of our everyday lives.

Something I hope to get out of this course is a better understanding of how AI works and how to use it effectively. AI first showed up in my life when I started using it to help correct my grammar when writing papers.

Since then, I have become more interested in understanding what is happening behind the technology instead of just using it.

One honest concern I have about AI is whether it will eventually lead to fewer jobs as more companies begin using AI to complete tasks that people currently do. I am interested in learning more about both the opportunities and challenges that AI could create.
